1 · The model

The model sees neither letters nor words. It sees these pieces, each one numbered in a vocabulary of about 128,000 entries. The same sentence costs 16 tokens in French and 10 in English, because the vocabulary was learned mostly on English. Domain words get cut into pieces: net + list, Ver + ilog. Numbers are split one digit or three digits at a time. The "next word" of the two previous slides is really the next token. The token is also the unit used to measure the context, which comes later.
