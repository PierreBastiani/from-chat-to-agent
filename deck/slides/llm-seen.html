What it has seen, what it has never seen

Seen in training

  • Verilog, Verilog-AMS
  • Netlists
  • Assembly
  • Python, C, Tcl
  • Public standards and datasheets

Never seen

  • Your proprietary verification language
  • Your internal communication language
  • Your specs and your design databases
  • Your testbenches and their conventions

What it has never seen, it makes up with confidence. For it to know, you have to put it in the text it reads.

1 · The model

This is the thread that runs through the whole talk. Everything that follows (the chat, the tools, the skills) is about putting the right information in the text at the right time. Example to give: the model already reads Verilog correctly, but it will make up the syntax of the internal verification language.
